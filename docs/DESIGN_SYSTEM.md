# HATOBA GROUP — DESIGN SYSTEM & VISUAL IDENTITY

## 1. Design Philosophy: "Institutional Trust & Culinary Vitality"
The HATOBA brand architecture reconciles corporate gravitas with fresh, appetizing food commerce:
1. **The Corporate Persona (HATOBA Group):**
   - High institutional trust, restrained luxury, editorial typography, structured grids, metallic gold accents on deep obsidian and dark emerald.
2. **The Commercial Persona (HATOBA Frozen Food & Culinary):**
   - Warm alabaster white canvases, crisp product packaging visuals, hygienic cold-chain indicators (-18°C), and focused crimson action points for conversions.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

- **Headings & Institutional Statements:**
  - `Cinzel` or `Playfair Display` serif with fallback to `Georgia, serif`.
  - Conveys prestige, heritage, and holding group solidity.
- **Body & Technical Specs:**
  - `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` with fallback to `system-ui, sans-serif`.
  - High legibility across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with optimal Indonesian diacritics support.

## 4. UI Components & Patterns

1. **Sticky Dual-Persona Header:**
   - Left: Gold Monogram HATOBA Group logo mark with brand name.
   - Center: Quick navigation (Tentang Kami, Ekosistem Bisnis, Peta Jalan, Kemitraan, Lokasi).
   - Right: Dual CTAs ("Katalog B2B" with gold outline & "Hubungi Korporat" with solid gold/crimson fill).
2. **Interactive Corporate Footprint (Indonesia Map):**
   - Visual map highlighting Bali (HQ & Production Hub) and Yogyakarta (Distribution Hub), with dashed roadmap pulsing to Bandung (2027 Expansion).
3. **Bento Grid for 4 Business Pillars:**
   - 4 asymmetric cards featuring status badges (`Aktif & Beroperasi`, `Tahap Lanjutan`, `Ekspansi Hulu`, `Divisi Finansial`).
   - Interactive hover cards with instant detailed modal / link routing.
4. **Horizontal Strategic Roadmap (2024-2027+):**
   - Elegant timeline with gold milestones: Phase 1 (B2B Dominance), Phase 2 (Culinary Retail), Phase 3 (Bandung Hub 2027), Phase 4 (Agro & Assets).
5. **B2B Frozen Food Commerce Engine:**
   - Authentic packaging photos, tabbed variant selection (Retail 240g/300g vs Partai/Wholesale), instant cart drawer, and validation prior to WhatsApp transfer.
6. **Smart Multi-Channel Inquiry Form:**
   - Dropdown categorizing intent: *Pemesanan B2B / HORECA*, *Kemitraan Restoran*, *Kerja Sama Pemasok/Peternakan*, *Hubungan Korporat/Investasi*.
   - Generates unique inquiry references (`HTB-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`).
